How to play
  • Choose Rock, Paper or Scissors. After a 3-2-1 countdown, the computer reveals its pick.
  • Rock beats Scissors, Scissors beats Paper, Paper beats Rock.
  • Same choice is a draw. Click Next Round to continue.
  • First to 5 wins takes the match, or click End Game to finish early.
